## Scope of the rights check

Public Domain Mark is a status label, not a Creative Commons attribution licence. The Commons file pages also note that reuse of faithful PD-Art reproductions can vary by jurisdiction; the table reports their classifications rather than universal legal clearance. The CC licence in a wiki page footer covers page text and must not be assumed to cover its image.

For the Dalí work, MoMA names Salvador Dalí, the Gala-Salvador Dalí Foundation and Artists Rights Society (ARS), New York in its current copyright credit. Wikipedia's rationale is specific to its article and US-hosted use. A separate permission or applicable lawful exception would need to cover this site's use; this URL fix does not establish that basis. The requested picture has been retained, with this exception made explicit for review before any decision to publish the change.
