Made for every way of learning

Children with dyslexia, low vision, ADHD or English as an additional language use the same app as everyone else, just set up their way.

  • Hear every word

    Lessons, questions and feedback read aloud, with a natural voice option that works on the device.
  • Aa

    Easy-to-read text

    A typeface designed for low vision, bigger text, wider spacing and a calm background tint.

  • No pressure

    No countdown timers unless a child turns one on, and mistakes are a chance to have another go.

  • See it your way

    Dark mode, high contrast and less motion, plus full keyboard and screen reader support.

How a session works

  1. 1

    MiRa suggests what to do next, or children choose any subject themselves.
  2. 2

    Learn in small steps

    A short lesson they can read, listen to, or both, one idea per screen.
  3. 3

    Try a quick quiz

    Questions adapt to how they are doing, with hints and kind feedback.
