Part 2 of 5 · How to find a fraction of a number

Share it out, then count

To find a fraction of a number, we share the number into equal groups.

The bottom number of the fraction tells us how many groups to make. (reading now)

Then we count what is in one group.

¼
Word to knowDenominator: the bottom number in a fraction. In ¼ the denominator is 4.

Example: what is ¼ of 20?

  1. The denominator is 4, so make 4 equal groups.
  2. 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so each group has 5.
  3. One group is one quarter.
¼ of 20 = 5
Remember: divide by the bottom number. For ¾ of 20, find one quarter (5), then take 3 of them: 15.
