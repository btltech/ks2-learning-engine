Fractions of amounts

Share it out, then count

To find a fraction of a number, we share the number into equal groups.

The bottom number tells us how many groups to make. (reading now)

Denominator: the bottom number in a fraction.
¼ of 20 = 5because 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
