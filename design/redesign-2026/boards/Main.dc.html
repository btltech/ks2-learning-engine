Clear Path

One thing at a time, said clearly, readable and hearable by every child. These foundations apply to every screen in the KS2 app.

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum Ages 7–11

Six promises

Readable first Atkinson Hyperlegible, 18px or more for reading, 1.6 line height, lines no longer than 65 characters.
Hear anything A Listen button on every lesson, question and message. A natural voice if the family turns it on.
Words with every icon No mystery buttons. Colour never carries meaning alone: every state also has an icon and words.
Calm, never rushed No countdowns unless a child chooses one. Mistakes say “have another go”, never a red cross.
Big and forgiving Touch targets at least 48px, generous spacing, a clear Back on every screen, and nothing lost by a mis-tap.
Your way Text size, spacing, a calm background tint, dark mode, high contrast and less motion, saved per child.
