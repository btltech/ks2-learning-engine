Rules that keep it friendly for everyone

  • Text is always ink on light colours. Bright colours are fills, never text.
  • Body text 18px or more, Atkinson Hyperlegible. Headings in Fredoka.
  • Every button is at least 48px tall, with a 3px outline and a chunky shadow.
  • Right and wrong always come with a word and an icon, never colour alone.
  • Wura the owl always appears with words, so no one has to guess.
  • All movement stops when “Less movement” is on. No timers unless chosen.
  • Dark, high contrast and calm modes still apply from Voice and display.
